# Mission and scope

**Status:** AGREED
**Last updated:** 2026-09-13
**Decision records:** [260913 — Adopt legal name and mission statement](../decisions/260913-legal-name-and-mission-statement.md)

## Mission statement

> Downtown Loyal Heights stewards the Loyal Heights neighborhood of Seattle, ensuring that growth is shaped by, governed by, and benefits the people who live, work, and gather there for generations.

## Notes on scope

- **Not tied to one transaction.** The mission is neighborhood stewardship, not the acquisition of the parcels at 80th & 24th specifically. If that deal falls through, the entity still has work to do: convening, educating, publishing the model, advocating in planning processes, holding covenants or easements, or partnering with whoever does buy.
- **"The people who live, work, and gather there."** Deliberately broader than "residents." Includes people who come to Loyal Heights for work, school, medical care, shopping, and everything else, and neighbors in adjacent small neighborhoods (especially Sunset Hill). Leaves the exact neighborhood boundary a board discussion rather than a legal definition.
- **Distinct from the Articles' purposes clause.** The formal 501(c)(3)/(c)(4) purposes clause in the Articles of Incorporation is separate, counsel-drafted language (e.g., something like "to promote social welfare and combat deterioration of the Loyal Heights neighborhood of Seattle"). This mission statement is the public-facing one; the two don't need to be word-for-word identical.
